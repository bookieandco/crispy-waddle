## Dedupe and weighting law

- Repetition does not increase personality weight.
- No single secondary source can self-promote into durable Personality.
- Durable changes still require the existing evidence, eligibility, and Personality
  admission path.
- Owner-authored material is not automatically more durable merely because it is
  personally relevant.
- The namesake is an anchor for expression style, not factual authority.
