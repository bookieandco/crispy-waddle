## Core rules admitted

### 1. Advertising accelerates; it does not create proof from nothing
Paid media is downstream of evidence that the offer, audience, destination, and creative can already produce a qualified response.

The implementation translates this into three explicit states:

`ORGANIC_PROOF_REQUIRED -> BOUNDED_PAID_TEST_READY -> PAID_ACCELERATION_READY`

The gate evaluates:
- qualified organic conversions;
- repeated winning content evidence;
- shared-media evidence;
- an owned destination;
- consented owned-audience capture and nurture readiness;
- known customer economics;
- maximum acceptable acquisition cost relative to contribution economics.

The numeric proof thresholds are configurable. They are operating defaults, not universal marketing laws.

### 1A. Auction readiness is provider-specific

The supplied advertising transcript also emphasizes three auction inputs: how much the advertiser is willing/able to pay, how likely the user is to take the desired action, and ad quality/relevance.

The implementation does **not** hard-code one universal auction equation because Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Amazon and other providers rank ads differently and may change their systems over time.

Instead, paid acceleration now requires provider-specific evidence for:
- audience/message fit;
- creative/ad quality;
- estimated or observed action-rate quality;
- enough approved learning budget to give the bounded test a fair chance to collect evidence;
- acceptable negative-feedback quality signals where the provider exposes them.

A bounded paid test may be used to collect this provider-specific evidence. A campaign cannot graduate to `PAID_ACCELERATION_READY` without it.

The learning budget is an explicit experiment/provider input, not a universal dollar amount. Jhadina must never infer that "spend more" is automatically the answer to poor creative, poor targeting, weak product-market proof, or bad economics.

### 2. PESO is a portfolio, not a paid-first funnel
The system treats:
- **Paid** as a governed accelerator after evidence;
- **Earned** as relationships, referrals, mentions, citations, partnerships, and trust transfer to cultivate;
- **Shared** as organic social/community distribution and observation;
- **Owned** as the durable priority: websites, landing pages, permissioned email/direct audience, and durable content.

Stopping paid spend must not erase the business's audience relationship. Social followers alone are not classified as owned audience.

### 3. Customer language is evidence
Content strategy should learn pains, desires, objections, and questions from attributable customer/public evidence such as:
- customer interviews;
- first-party support;
- reviews;
- public comments/forums;
- search questions.

The system must not invent pain points or infer sensitive traits to improve targeting.

### 4. Concentrate acquisition, then repurpose natively
A brand/offer should have one primary acquisition surface at a time when concentration improves learning and operating quality.

Selection is evidence-based, using:
- audience fit;
- operator/production fit;
- discovery longevity;
- native-format fit;
- qualified commercial evidence.

Secondary surfaces may receive derivatives, but blind identical cross-posting is not the policy. Platform choice must be relearned from outcomes rather than treated as permanent.

### 5. Brand content and conversion content are different jobs
The content system requires both:
- **brand content**: trust, familiarity, story, education, entertainment, identity, usefulness;
- **conversion content**: explains how the offer solves a problem and gives a relevant reason to act.

Every planned item has a job, CTA type, destination, audience-signal lineage, and evidence.

### 6. Owned capture is part of the content system
The strategy can bind:
- an owned destination;
- a lead magnet / small useful first win;
- explicit-consent capture;
- a nurture sequence;
- continuing permissioned relationship.

Purchased/scraped lists are not admitted. Unsubscribe must be honored.

### 7. Faceless does not mean identity-less
Faceless creative remains supported. When a plan is faceless it must carry a recognizable visual identity reference rather than defaulting to interchangeable stock/B-roll patterns.

This complements Director's product/style/character identity systems.

## Source claims treated as hypotheses, not hard-coded facts

The supplied transcripts also included:
- specific platform-ad revenue figures;
- specific claims about email effectiveness;
- specific posting-frequency prescriptions;
- named vendors/tools and affiliate promotions;
- universal-sounding statements about individual platforms;
- anecdotes about creator/business revenue.

Those claims were **not** imported as factual policy.

Where useful, the system converts them into measurable hypotheses:
- compare organic vs paid qualified acquisition;
- measure channel-specific conversion and contribution;
- learn cadence per niche/platform;
- test lead-capture and nurture performance;
- compare search-longevity vs high-volume social distribution;
- measure the actual value of earned/shared/owned media for each venture.

## Authority boundaries

This fold changes strategy/admission only.

It does not grant:
- public publication authority;
- public-comment authority;
- DM/outreach authority;
- paid spend authority;
- budget-increase authority;
- contract/rights authority;
- provider credential authority.

Paid execution still requires:
1. Growth paid-ad plan and exact fingerprint;
2. SOCIAL-JUGGERNAUT.25 proof-before-paid admission;
3. exact weekly packet approval;
4. single-use delegated child permit;
5. Action Core policy/receipt verification;

7. realized-outcome measurement before additional scale.
