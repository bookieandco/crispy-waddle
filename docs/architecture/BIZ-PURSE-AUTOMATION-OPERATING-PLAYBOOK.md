## Added code

`packages/opportunity-core/src/domain/biz-purse-business-automation-playbook.ts` and matching regression tests; exported and wired into Opportunity Core CI. It is deliberately pure and non-executing:

1. Intake from anonymous/pseudonymous `lead:` identifiers, `fixture:` evidence, scoped business ownership, no raw addresses, phone numbers or private conversations.
2. Lead-fit score of documented need, service fit, timing and budget/decision authority dimensions, **never age/demographic or sensitive profiling**. Threshold yields an internal human-review recommendation only; not an automated exclusion or discriminatory decision.
3. An inbound service request may produce an **initial reply draft**. A follow, story view or comment is not consent to a marketing DM, SMS or phone call. Marketing follow-ups require separate explicit opt-in evidence, unsuppressed contact, a recorded initial reply and no subsequent inbound reply. Proposed schedule is 24h, 3d, 7d, 14d, 21d from the recorded first reply, at most one next draft per cycle; five total is a ceiling, **not** a mandate to contact anyone.
4. Onboarding first-week small-win review and 72-hour inactivity review; verified customer status is still an untrusted field absent an authenticated order/payment service.
5. Routine support draft review; refunds, complaints and safety escalated to human handling, no autogenerated financial promises.
6. Content draft approval requires brand/rights review and separate publisher commissioning.
7. Daily cash snapshot review is informational; unresolved exceptions and missing external reconciliation cannot create spendable balance.
8. Deterministic replay/digest, owner stop, evidence and timestamp guards. All tasks set `humanApprovalRequired=true` and `canSend/canCall/canPublish/canCharge=false`; the overall plan keeps `canMoveMoney/canPurchaseSoftware/canTreatClaimsAsROI=false`.

Commercial email must be checked for sender identification, truthful content, postal address and opt-out before any send, including B2B messages; the FTC explains that requirements apply beyond bulk marketing: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business. Platform DM windows, messaging permissions, phone/SMS opt-in, opt-outs, approved sender identities, logging and privacy deletion require independent per-channel review. **Do not impersonate a human** to hide AI generation.

## Performance measurements to implement only after authorization

- Lead count **with explicit permission**, not follower count alone
- Inquiry-to-first-response delay and abandonment
- Qualification precision by verified client outcomes, with no sensitive-category proxy scoring
- Response and booking rates, conversion and complaint/opt-out rates
- Days to first customer value; support escalation and resolution time
- Content rights approvals, verified engagement and actual campaign cost
- Provider-settled revenue net of fees/refunds/tax provisions, cash coverage and gross vs actual profit
- Idempotency, delivery failure, worker downtime and human intervention count

The videos' dollar examples (including “make a million,” high consulting fees, agency earnings and near-zero media costs) are illustrative marketing statements and MUST NOT populate Jhadina's claimed business-performance or ROI fields.
