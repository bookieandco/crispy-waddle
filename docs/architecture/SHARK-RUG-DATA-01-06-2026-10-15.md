## .05 Prospective controls / holdout contract

`evaluate_sealed_holdout` accepts **separately prepared**, prospectively sealed cases with a case ID, actor group, chain, RUG or CONTROL outcome, pre-frozen binary signal, observed feature availability time, decision time, later outcome availability, and independent outcome provenance. It rejects:

- actor overlap with known training groups, duplicate identities, non-HOLDOUT predictions;
- leaked future labels or features, rule freezes after prediction;
- identical predictor/outcome source, mixed-chain pooled scores;
- missing or inadequate negative controls.

The default minimum is 10 positives and 10 controls from at least two distinct outcome sources to get a **descriptive** balanced accuracy, TPR and FPR; anything weaker is explicitly **UNSCORED**. This is a test contract, **not** a guarantee of calibration, predictive validity, representative sampling, or production readiness. Real prospective cases must be acquired separately, with defensible source independence. No real cases or empirical performance metrics are claimed by this PR.

## .06 Canonical SHARK bridge and boundaries

`rug-research-bridge.ts` converts a timestamped, pinned EVM historical research hypothesis into the **existing** `RugModelSignal` shape with calibration locked to `UNVERIFIED`; Solana is always `OUT_OF_DOMAIN`. It never generates `PAPER_CALIBRATED` or `SHADOW_CALIBRATED`. It rejects lookahead and malformed inputs. Existing `evaluateRugSelfProtection` ignores unverified/out-of-domain model escalation and always preserves deterministic quarantine and missing-evidence fail-closure. The bridge is exported for research consumers **but is not automatically wired into a live caller**.
