## Six markets and eight sport categories

- All 8 sport categories can be discovered: NFL, NCAAF, NBA, NHL, MLB, soccer, tennis, UFC. A forecast only appears when sufficient pre-event historical evidence exists; UFC or other sports may have **zero qualifying forecasts**.
- Winner: research-only uncalibrated probability. Soccer uses a three-way draw option. Tennis and UFC records use **match-win indicators**, not basketball/football-style points.
- Spread and game total: score-based research projections only; **no verified market line**.
- Player props, period props, touchdowns/scorers: **blocked**, pending licensed feeds, verified player/period statistics, and settlement rules.
- `markets.mjs` defines all six categories and rejects absent rights and unsourced quotes. It does not invent odds.
- No official event/score/settlement certification; provisional grades are marked `PRELIMINARY_UNOFFICIAL_NOT_SETTLED`, not used for training.
- Models have no out-of-sample calibration certification. Probability-vs-score contradictions are flagged for owner review; original forecasts remain immutable.

### Failure policy

Missing/malformed data → **abstain**, not forecast. Missing odds → **no market comparison**, never synthetic quotes. Conflicting grades → **exclude pending review**. Provider total outage → workflow **fails** with diagnostic artifact and **does not publish forecasts**. Existing ledger file conflict → **fails without overwriting**. Late prediction → **rejected**. Real wagering → **always disabled**.

This handoff records verified capabilities, not a claim that all six market integrations or official settlements have been completed.
