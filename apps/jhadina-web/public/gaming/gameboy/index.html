Jhadina · Game Boy

Local cartridge · Offline-first · Phone landscape

Choose a local cartridge to begin. Emulator assets must be approved and installed on this server.

The ROM stays in this browser’s IndexedDB and in the emulator’s in-memory blob URL. Gameplay requires approved, self-hosted EmulatorJS files; no CDN fallback. Emulator controls support touch and compatible gamepads where the browser permits. Save-state callbacks are preserved locally when emitted by the runtime. Browser storage can still be cleared by the device.
