## .12: WATCHKEEPER convergence

`ops/meme/rug_watchkeeper_fusion.py` consumes existing:

1. `rug_native_shadow.assess_solana_evidence` for the existing Solana mint and pool-vault evidence;
2. `token_authority_forensics.audit_mint_account` for Token/Token-2022 mint extensions;
3. `dual_rpc_pool_observer.compare` receipts for pinned *public host* same-slot agreement;
4. `paired_route_evidence.evaluate` for a genuine SHA-chained *local event ledger* of buy and reverse GET quotes;
5. `paired_route_cost_stress.model` for explicit hypothetical quote-cost sensitivity.

No collector, scheduler, executor, wallet, network endpoint, or database pipeline was added. The feature never accepts arbitrary claimed "verified sellability"; the route is recomputed by the canonical hash-chain evaluator.

Receipt identity and digests are checked across module boundaries, along with explicit NO_TRADE permission booleans, mint/pool continuity, nonfuture cutoff, and maximum 30-second freshness for the dual-RPC receipt.

Possible outputs: `QUARANTINE_CONFLICTED_EVIDENCE`, `REVIEW_RISK_CONTROLS`, `ABSTAIN_INCOMPLETE_EVIDENCE`, or `WATCH_UNCERTIFIED_NO_TRADE`. None means a token is safe, proven fraudulent, authorized for paper fill or approved for live entry/exit.

## .13: chain-native evidence integrity

The existing WATCHKEEPER.12 compares two **public** nodes' one-slot raw pool and vault account images. A match is consistency, **not** independence or proof of Solana finality. No RPC archive proof, signed bank state, complete LP withdrawal trail, ownership certification or executed sell route exists in this PR.

The fusion refuses mismatched token/pool identity, stale/future dual snapshots, unrecognized receipt statuses, a fake affirmative execution flag, tampered SHA receipts, forged mint-provenance links, future Jupiter quote events, and a mismatch between forward and reversed output amount. It additionally compares dual-host atomic vault reserves and slot numbers against the canonical SHARK pool proof; self-consistent two-host agreement on different balances or older slots is explicitly quarantined. Unobserved evidence fails closed.

The optional cost stress is tied only to the canonical paired-quote digest. Double-counting embedded aggregator route fees remains possible; the result is not realized or simulated executable profit.

## .14: SPL Token-2022 controls

`ops/meme/token_authority_forensics.py` analyzes explicitly provided `getAccountInfo(jsonParsed)` mint data via the existing `solana_risk_probe.verify_mint` parser. It records active mint/freeze authorities, transfer hooks, transfer-fee configs, default frozen state, permanent delegates, nontransferability, pause/config control, confidentiality, close authority, and unknown/unsupported extensions. Unknown extensions are escalated as a research visibility gap. Token-2022 extensions omitted by a provider remain UNKNOWN, not safe.

This is **not** a full on-chain Token-2022 extension decoder. Source-provided parsed extensions may omit or misrepresent state, and account-level restrictions still require independent account reads and (in later work) safe transaction simulation. A transfer-hook warning is not proof of an actual transfer failure. A clean mint metadata inspection cannot certify sellability or a safe token.
