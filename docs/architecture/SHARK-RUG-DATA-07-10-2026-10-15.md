## .07 — Chain-native evidence

`ops/meme/rug_native_shadow.py:assess_solana_evidence` **reuses** the existing `solana_risk_probe.verify_mint` and `pool_vault_probe.attest_pool` output formats rather than adding network calls or a parallel blockchain collector.

- Validate Solana base58 public keys as 32 bytes, known Token/Token-2022 mint program IDs, PumpSwap/Raydium AMM V4 program identity, pool/vault/mint continuity, compatible slots, and `observedAt <= availableAt <= informationCutoff`.
- Reject mismatched vault tokens, stale pools, unsupported programs, false rug conclusions, and executable authority.
- Active mint/freeze authorities trigger **research review**, not proven fraud.
- Even complete mint+vault snapshots cannot certify sellability, LP control, actor independence, real exit liquidity or token safety. A missing critical receipt produces `ABSTAIN`.
- All receipt digests are local content hashes; **not** independently authenticated on-chain receipts or external time witnesses.

## .08 — Independently monitored negative-control design

`seal_control_cohort` prespecifies every case, actor group, venue stratum, discovery timestamp, minimum control horizon (>=24 h), and sampling policy `PROSPECTIVE_ALL_ELIGIBLE`. No hindsight sampling, duplicate actor groups or training-group overlap.

A later outcome classified `MONITORED_NO_REPORTED_RUG` is **only an observed monitoring window**, not a lifetime safety certification. The grader requires a complete prespecified horizon and at least two stated non-identical source families and distinct source evidence identifiers. These asserted sources still require *independent external verification*. A missing outcome remains censored; a price drop alone cannot become a `REPORTED_LP_WITHDRAWAL` verdict.

## .09 — Leak-resistant historical replay

`freeze_shadow_decisions` requires an untampered source-only cohort seal, exactly one prospective decision for every prespecified case, and real feature availability timestamps at or after the seal and no later than the decision. It retains the normalized proof digest and fail-closed abstentions.

`grade_shadow_replay` admits only future outcome claims after the frozen decision, as-of cutoff and source checks. It detects unknown, missing and pending outcomes, seal/decision hash changes, changed case identity and unsupported classifications. Deterministic SHA-256 hashes make **local state tamper-evident if hashes are anchored externally**, but do not prove actual prospective capture.

## .10 — Shadow evaluation

The scorer reuses `rug_dataset_research.evaluate_sealed_holdout`, whose descriptive balanced accuracy/TPR/FPR threshold requires at least 10 rug-reported examples, 10 monitored controls and two outcome source families. If any prespecified case abstains or is censored, the cohort reports `UNSCORED_INCOMPLETE`: no cherry-picking away hard samples. Scored outputs are only `DESCRIPTIVE_REPORTED_OUTCOMES_ONLY` with `calibration: UNVERIFIED`, never `PAPER_CALIBRATED`, `SHADOW_CALIBRATED`, money authority, actual fills or certified profit.

This component is an **offline research scorecard**, not the canonical durable SHARK Shadow scheduler/repository. It does not bypass or automatically modify `ops/meme/shadow_evidence_bridge.py`, `ops/meme/shadow_sealer.py`, canonical Money risk, or Watchkeeper proposal approval. Runtime commissioning is separate.
