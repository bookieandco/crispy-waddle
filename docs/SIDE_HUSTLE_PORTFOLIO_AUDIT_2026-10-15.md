## Mostly autonomous candidates (eventual, conditional)
1. **Research briefs**: source discovery, original synthesis, citations, QA, report packaging and receipt logging. Owner approves client commitment; factual accuracy/rights checked.
2. **SEO/AEO audits**: crawl and query research, prioritized change plan, original conversion copy and monitored reports. Changes to a customer's site require scoped authorization.
3. **Boring-business services**: spreadsheet cleansing, repetitive reconciliations and SOPs. Protect customer PII and require acceptance.
4. **Creative/media service packages**: original licensed thumbnails, short-form variants, visualizers, social asset bundles. Human rights/QC review; publishing is separately authorized.
5. **Digital products**: original templates, study guides, planners and downloadable workbooks. Rights/product QA then governed listing, payment and delivery.
6. **Website diagnostics, authorized social scheduling and affiliate evidence monitoring**: automated internal reporting possible; site changes, posts and promotional claims need approvals and provider-bound receipts.
Do not describe any as an autonomous money-making business until validated purchases/customers, permissions, exception handling and measured margin exist. Treat human premium services, physical custody, bidding, money movement and trading as unsuitable for full unattended execution.
