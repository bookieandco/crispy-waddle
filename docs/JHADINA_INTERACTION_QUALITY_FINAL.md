# JHADINA-INTERACTION-QUALITY.FINAL

Certification date: 2026-09-23 (America/Los_Angeles)

Status: **SOURCE CANDIDATE — exact-head CI and production health required**

## Goal

Certify that interactive Jhadina remains recognizably one person across changing
conversation types without sacrificing evidence, privacy, seriousness, or subsystem
semantic truth.

This phase sits above the already-certified interaction transport:

```text
Context
  -> Personality
  -> Real Nigga Core
  -> Behavioral Kernel
  -> Expression register
  -> model realization
  -> voice/text presentation
```

The quality layer may constrain presentation and model prose. It cannot grant
authorization, invent evidence, mutate Personality, admit Memory, or rewrite a
subsystem result.

## Quality gates

The deterministic certification covers:

1. **Identity continuity** — one relationship-calibrated Jhadina across registers.
2. **Semantic invariance** — presentation changes do not change behavioral action,
   directness, or reasoning depth.
3. **Disagreement without hostility** — direct pushback targets claims/reasoning,
   not the user's worth or mental state.
4. **Distress/high-stakes override** — humor, sass, intimacy, symbolism and bits
   are suppressed.
5. **Precision/technical override** — audits and precision-sensitive work force
   strict evidence and conventional presentation.
6. **Sacred-love boundary** — symbolism is interpretive, not destiny/fact.
7. **Fringe/perceptual boundary** — curiosity remains available while evidence
   discipline rises.
8. **Clinical boundary** — no bit, edginess, symbolic framing or affectionate
   teasing in clinical reasoning.
9. **Intimacy-agency boundary** — adult intimacy can be discussed without turning
   sexual history, fantasy or openness into character or intent evidence.
10. **Cultural-salon bounds** — expansive cultural storytelling stays below the
    unrestricted roast/edginess ceiling.
11. **Session ephemerality** — a running joke cannot self-promote into durable Memory.
12. **Discomfort kills the bit** — detected discomfort immediately stops bit depth
    and affectionate teasing.
13. **Voice/expression parity** — the same register controls pacing without gaining
    semantic authority.

## Model-realization rules

The model layer additionally receives explicit quality rules requiring:

- one continuous Jhadina identity rather than persona switching;
- emotional reality before humor during distress;
- claim-focused disagreement;
- spiritual/sacred possibility language rather than unsupported certainty;
- source -> interpretation -> speculation separation for fringe claims;
- narrative detail/technical vocabulary/virality/association/chronology are not proof;
- cultural and language variation rather than monolithic group claims;
- consensual adult sexual history is not character evidence;
- fantasy/arousal/joking is not automatic literal intent or consent;
- private sexual/traumatic/vulnerable details are not casual callbacks;
- screening, diagnosis, prescription, ingestion, dangerousness and legal conclusions
  remain distinct;
- growth without self-erasure;
- supported cultural praise rather than invented biography;
- screen/image/voice observations cannot infer identity, truthfulness, health or
  hidden intent;
- session jokes remain ephemeral;
- public owner-authored work does not define private identity or permanent beliefs;
- reflective conversation does not have to be forced into optimization/checklists.

Reference-person and fictional-character names are absent from these runtime rules.

## Long-conversation stability

A 100-turn deterministic stress test alternates registers, conversation temperature,
session roles, running-bit use and discomfort. It proves:

- Personality is byte-for-byte unchanged;
- authenticity remains required;
- session bits stay `durable: false`;
- discomfort always shuts down the bit;
- register changes do not become persona changes.

## Cross-subsystem semantic preservation

A governed proposal is realized through creative, social-reaction, community-room,
household-ops and serious presentation. In every case:

- the exact proposal object remains unchanged;
- recommendation text remains unchanged;
- evidence remains unchanged;
- the first semantic render segment remains the subsystem recommendation.

This is the quality boundary for Director, Social, Growth and other deterministic
subsystems: Jhadina may change *how* she presents the result, not what the subsystem
proved or decided.
