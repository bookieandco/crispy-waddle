# SH reference audit — AI dropshipping build-to-exit model

Date: 2026-09-21
Disposition: HIGH_VALUE_STRATEGY_REFERENCE
Execution impact: roadmap / metrics / governance; not provider truth

## Core useful thesis

The transcript's durable contribution is a build-to-exit operating model:

1. select evergreen demand rather than purely novelty-driven demand;
2. build a multi-product branded catalog;
3. acquire customers through intent-capture channels such as Google Shopping/Search;
4. optimize for actual profit, not top-line revenue;
5. reduce founder dependency through automation, delegation, and SOPs;
6. keep clean accrual/cash-basis records and transferable operating evidence;
7. maintain buyer-visible growth levers;
8. value the business from trailing earnings rather than assuming revenue equals value;
9. eventually sell the asset and recycle capital into additional brands.

This should inform Jhadina's side-hustle strategy without becoming a hard-coded promise about margins, conversion rates, traffic, valuation multiples, or exit timing.

## Claims to treat as hypotheses, not facts

- third-party traffic estimates are not verified order/revenue data;
- assumed 3%, 4–6%, or other conversion rates cannot prove store revenue;
- assumed 25–35% net margins cannot prove store profit;
- a store being advertised heavily on Google does not prove Google is its only profitable channel;
- product cost comparisons from AliExpress do not establish identical quality, shipping, compliance, branding, or landed cost;
- "Google is the only way" is strategy opinion, not an architectural rule;
- "Q1 is store-selling season" is not a certification criterion;
- 2x/3x/etc. exit multiples must come from current market evidence and actual business quality.

## Jhadina architecture addition — Brand Exit Readiness

Do not create another execution authority.

Opportunity / Commerce / Growth / Money / Attribution should feed a derived, evidence-backed brand-exit readiness view.

### Exit-readiness dimensions

**1. Profit quality**
- TTM revenue
- TTM net profit / SDE / EBITDA where appropriate
- gross margin
- net margin
- contribution margin
- refund/chargeback rate
- COGS volatility
- ad spend dependence
- cash conversion / working-capital burden

**2. Demand durability**
- search-demand stability
- seasonality
- branded vs non-branded demand
- product concentration
- catalog concentration
- repeat-purchase rate
- cohort retention where applicable

**3. Channel durability**
- Google Shopping/Search contribution
- SEO contribution
- direct/repeat contribution
- email/SMS contribution
- paid-social contribution
- channel concentration risk
- CAC, ROAS/MER, conversion rate, AOV by channel

**4. Operational transferability**
- founder hours/week
- percentage of workflows documented
- percentage automated
- percentage delegated
- number of single-person dependencies
- supplier concentration
- supplier account transferability
- customer-support workload
- fulfillment exception rate

**5. Brand / defensibility**
- owned domain
- trademark/IP status
- branded creative/product imagery
- packaging/customization
- exclusive/private-supplier arrangements where real
- reviews/customer list
- repeat customer base
- differentiated product bundles/content

**6. Growth-option evidence**
- untested geographies
- untested channels
- catalog expansion paths
- cross-sell/upsell paths
- adjacent-store/brand opportunities
- organic/SEO opportunity
- email/SMS monetization opportunity

**7. Due-diligence readiness**
- clean P&L
- cash-basis and accrual reconciliation where relevant
- ad-spend reconciliation
- bank/payment processor reconciliation
- supplier invoices
- tax records
- refund/chargeback records
- inventory/reservation records
- SOP repository
- credential/account transfer plan
- material-contract inventory

## Derived outputs

Jhadina may derive:
- ExitReadinessSnapshot
- FounderDependencySnapshot
- ChannelConcentrationSnapshot
- BuyerGrowthLevers
- DueDiligenceChecklist
- ValuationEvidenceRange

These must be evidence-backed summaries, not guaranteed valuations.

## Valuation rule

Use current marketplace/broker evidence for valuation ranges.

Do not hard-code a single multiple. Candidate estimates should preserve:
- valuation basis (TTM net profit / SDE / EBITDA / revenue when appropriate);
- source and observation date;
- comparable business type;
- size band;
- growth/stability notes;
- owner workload;
- concentration risks;
- defensibility;
- confidence/range.

## Google demand / ad intelligence placement

Opportunity / Growth:
- Google Trends -> demand trend evidence;
- Keyword Planner -> keyword/search-volume evidence;
- Merchant Center product feed -> current product data;
- Google Ads conversion tracking -> actual channel outcomes;
- Search/Shopping query reports -> observed search demand and negative-keyword candidates;
- competitive intelligence -> competitor assortment, pricing, positioning.

Commerce:
- product/catalog truth;
- supplier/landed cost;
- availability/fulfillment;
- price change proposal.

Growth:
- campaign/budget/bid/feed optimization proposals.

Action Core:
- consequential budget changes, publishing, price changes, supplier procurement, or messaging remain governed actions.

Money Core:
- actual profit and cash truth.

Attribution:
- channel-level revenue/cost/performance.

## Bottom line

The useful system is not "pump a generic dropshipping store with Google." It is:

evergreen demand
-> branded catalog
-> verified unit economics
-> diversified/transferable acquisition
-> automated/documented operations
-> clean financial evidence
-> low founder dependence
-> current-market valuation evidence
-> optional exit.

That is the version Jhadina should operationalize.
