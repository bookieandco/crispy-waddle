## Audit verdict

The TikTok Shop work should **not** become a standalone business factory.

The repo's canonical doctrine already says:

`Opportunity Core -> Side Hustle Business Factory -> Venture Lab -> Side Hustle Experiment -> Outcome Learning -> Side Hustle Maturity`

TikTok therefore belongs in two places:

1. **upstream evidence + discovery**
   - TikTok Shop product/search/creator/offer/campaign/GMV-Max/LIVE observations become Venture market signals;
   - those signals synthesize ordinary Side Hustle discovery candidates;
   - candidates become canonical Opportunities through the existing Venture candidate adoption path.

2. **downstream execution adapters**
   - after research, MAKE IT MAKE SENSE, originality/IP and bounded validation pass;
   - Growth / Director / Commerce / PupsonStuff receive evidence-backed work;
   - publication, fulfillment and money truth remain owned by their canonical systems;
   - realized outcomes feed the existing Side Hustle Lab learning and maturity path.

There is no separate TikTok Opportunity authority, TikTok portfolio authority, TikTok outcome truth, or TikTok money authority.

## Business Factory lane ownership

### TikTok affiliate

Canonical Side Hustle family:

`commerce_affiliate`

Discovery seed:

`tiktok-affiliate-commerce-market`

Research evidence may include:
- product demand;
- recent GMV momentum;
- creator/content saturation;
- creator concentration;
- commission;
- new-release age;
- search opportunity;
- current offer;
- observed GMV-Max participation / authorization;
- creative commerce outcomes;
- LIVE outcomes.

Validation archetype remains the existing:

`affiliate_conversion`

Success remains based on attributable conversion evidence, not views or posting volume.

Realized revenue remains governed by the existing affiliate portfolio/contribution law:

`PAYOUT_EVENT_ONLY`

Approved or pending commission is not realized revenue.

### TikTok POD seller

Canonical Side Hustle family:

`pod_personalized_commerce`

Discovery seed:

`tiktok-pod-commerce-market`

A POD seller candidate additionally requires fulfillment evidence before Business Factory intake:
- sales-channel connection;
- product/channel eligibility;
- tax readiness;
- merchant/provider/destination region;
- tracking compatibility;
- provider destination support;
- handling SLA;
- Made-to-Order state where relevant.

Validation archetype remains the existing:

`product_preorder`

Modeled contribution is research/validation evidence only.

Realized business outcome now has a separate settlement proof that uses:
- sale settlement;
- refunds;
- chargebacks;
- platform fees;
- affiliate commission;
- promotion fees;
- fulfillment cost;
- shipping cost;
- experiment spend.

Only settlement evidence can create the seller-side realized contribution calculation.

## TikTok Product Sniper placement

Product Sniper is **not** a portfolio manager and does not bypass Venture Factory.

Correct role:

```text
TikTok observations
 -> Product Sniper ranking / filtering
 -> VentureMarketSignal
 -> candidate synthesis
 -> research + MAKE IT MAKE SENSE
 -> experiment
```

Product Sniper may rank:
- momentum;
- saturation;
- commission;
- search demand / supply;
- creator concentration;
- new-release timing;
- offer quality/freshness;
- historical seller paid-support evidence;
- creative feasibility;
- Product Truth / policy risk;
- realized contribution learned from prior experiments.

It does not:
- authorize publication;
- authorize spend;
- authorize account actions;
- treat projected GMV as revenue;
- bypass originality;
- bypass bounded validation.

## Director / creative placement

Director owns creative production, not distribution authority.

The TikTok fold provides Director:
- Product Truth Lock;
- approved claim refs;
- approved offer refs;
- evidence/rights-cleared overlays;
- synthetic creator truth restrictions;
- provider-neutral batch generation;
- short-form loop/timeline assembly;
- generation cost ceilings.

Reference videos are reduced to abstract mechanics:
- hook class;
- framing;
- motion language;
- pacing;
- demonstration pattern;
- CTA class.

Direct creative cloning remains unauthorized.

## Offer lifecycle

Time-sensitive creative is not evergreen.

`TikTokCreativeOfferBinding` binds published creative to current offer observations.

If an offer:
- expires;
- becomes inactive;
- becomes unknown;
- no longer matches the product;

the system may propose:
- retire content;
- regenerate without offer;
- manual review.

The evidence layer itself does not delete published content. Any consequential platform action still belongs to the governed publication/action path.

## Audit defects found and repaired

### 1. Durable Venture receipt schema drift

TypeScript runtime accepts these receipt kinds:

- market_scout
- supervisor
- spatial_projection
- research_completion
- validation_admission
- experiment_bridge
- validation_result
- outcome_bridge
- memory_commit
- maturity_assessment
- persona_projection
- live_final

The original database migration only allowed five of them.

That meant Side Hustle Lab could compile but fail when persisting newer receipts.

Repair:

`supabase/migrations/20261004082000_venture_runtime_receipt_kinds.sql`

The database constraint is now aligned with the canonical runtime taxonomy.

### 2. TikTok POD tests were not in Opportunity Core's explicit test script

Opportunity Core does not auto-discover all tests; its `package.json` enumerates them.

The TikTok POD tests are now registered:
- `tiktok-shop-pod.test.ts`
- `tiktok-pod-contribution.test.ts`

### 3. Affiliate outcome parity existed; seller parity did not

Affiliate already had:
- canonical affiliate event states;
- payout-only realized revenue;
- reversal accounting;
- experiment-spend accounting;
- contribution proof;
- OpportunityOutcome calculation.

The POD seller path previously stopped at modeled economics.

Repair:
- `tiktok-pod-contribution.ts`
- `tiktok-pod-contribution.test.ts`

Seller/POD now has evidence-backed realized contribution calculation from settlements.

## Remaining live gaps

The architecture is folded, but the business is **not** live-certified yet.

Still required:

1. authorized TikTok data adapters for:
   - account capability;
   - current product/offer state;
   - Product Opportunities;
   - campaign state;
   - Shop Ads / GMV-Max observations;
   - creative performance;
   - orders / affiliate commission / payouts;
   - seller settlements / refunds.

2. Printify -> `TikTokPodFulfillmentObservation` adapter using only provider facts the integration can actually prove.

3. Product Truth / offer / overlay evidence must be enforced in the final commercial QC route before generation/publication.

4. RunPod/local-first production should become a Director provider implementation, not new Business Factory authority.

5. publication must return durable receipt/evidence into Venture work.

6. seller settlement observations need a live adapter feeding `TikTokPodContributionProof`.

7. at least one real bounded TikTok affiliate experiment and one POD experiment are needed before family maturity or scale is earned.

8. live certification still requires realized outcomes and normal Side Hustle / Venture evidence.

## Final architecture rule

TikTok is now an **evidence source + execution surface** inside Jhadina's existing Business Factory.

It does not own the business lifecycle.

The invariant is:

`observe aggressively -> reason canonically -> validate cheaply -> execute through owning systems -> learn only from realized outcomes`.
